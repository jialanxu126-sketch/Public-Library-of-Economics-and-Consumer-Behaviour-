About the library

The approach
The papers are the starting point. The questions, connections and analysis are the work.

This project explores economics and consumer behaviour through academic research, with a focus on understanding what the evidence actually tells us.

Research is examined critically, connected to related ideas, and translated into questions and applications that can be understood beyond academia.

Findings, interpretations and hypotheses are kept distinct so that uncertainty and limitations remain visible.
