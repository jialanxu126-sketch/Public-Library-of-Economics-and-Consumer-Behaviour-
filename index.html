About the library

Methodology
The papers are the starting point. The questions, connections and analysis are the work.

This project is being developed as an independent research library exploring economics, consumer behaviour and related fields.

Academic research will be read, examined critically, connected to other evidence and translated into accessible analysis.

Published findings, interpretation, hypotheses and speculation will be kept distinct so that readers can understand what is established, what is inferred, and what remains uncertain.
